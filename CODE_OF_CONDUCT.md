# Community conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute, regardless of experience or background. Ask clear
questions, explain your reasoning, and keep feedback about the work rather than the
person. Respect other people's time and accept that maintainers may decline a change.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable. Never post another person's
mail, calendar details, credentials, or account identifiers, even in a bug report.

This applies to this project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
Mark Unthank moderates these spaces and may edit or remove content, close or lock a
conversation, warn participants, or block repeated or serious misconduct. Enforcement
should consider the severity, context, and impact of the behavior.

Use GitHub's **Report content** / **Report abuse** controls to report misconduct privately
to GitHub. Do not repeat private or harmful content in a public issue. For ordinary
repository moderation, flag the relevant conversation to the maintainer with only the
minimum public context needed. GitHub reports are handled by GitHub, not a private
project reporting inbox. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
